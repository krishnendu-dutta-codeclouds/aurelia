3:T5c3,When we launched Aurelia in 2024, we made three commitments on packaging: recyclability, ocean-bound plastic, and refillable formats. Two years in, here is where we stand.

**2026 Numbers**

- 87% of packaging components are now recyclable or compostable
- 63% of plastic components use ocean-bound reclaimed resin
- Our glass jars (Luna Cream line) are 100% post-consumer recycled glass
- CO₂ offset per unit shipped: 2.3 kg (up from 0.8 kg in 2024)

**What Still Needs Work**

The serum pump mechanism — the metal + plastic spring assembly — remains our biggest challenge. We are currently in final-stage testing with a bioplastic spring supplier in the Netherlands. Target: full transition by Q3 2027.

The outer cartons are currently 90% FSC-certified. The remaining 10% is in transition — our Taiwanese carton supplier received FSC certification in April 2026, and all new production runs use certified stock.

**The 2028 Target**

Zero plastic to landfill from primary packaging. Every component either: recycled, refillable, or composted in our takeback programme.

We launched the Takeback Programme in March 2026. In three months, we have received 4,200 returned units — that is 4,200 units diverted from general waste. Every returned unit is audited, sanitised, and either refilled for our internal sampling programme or shredded and returned to the supply chain as raw material.

We are not perfect yet.
