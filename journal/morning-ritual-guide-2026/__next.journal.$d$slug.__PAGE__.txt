**Step 01: Cleanse (Not included here, but essential)**
Always begin with a clean canvas. Hard water minerals and residual product form a film that blocks active ingredient penetration by up to 60%.

**Step 02: Botanical Harmony Toner (pH 5.5)**
Apply within 60 seconds of cleansing while the skin is still slightly damp. The Toner's pH 5.5 formula restores the acid mantle disrupted by cleansing, while Willow Bark Extract initiates gentle BHA exfoliation. This step is not optional — it primes the acid mantle for optimal serum absorption.

Wait: 30 seconds.

**Step 03: Aura Radiance Serum (pH 3.2)**
The serum's slightly acidic pH (3.2) drives THDC into the epidermis efficiently. Apply to slightly damp skin — a light hydration layer on the stratum corneum facilitates ingredient penetration. Use 3–4 drops, pressing (never dragging) upward from neck to forehead.

Wait: 60 full seconds. This is the most skipped step and the most important. Rushing to layer moisturiser before the serum has absorbed will dilute its concentration and raise the effective pH, reducing efficacy.

**Step 04: Luna Repair Cream (if used AM)**
If using in the morning, apply a pea-sized amount as the final leave-on layer before SPF. Warm between fingertips to activate the lipid complex.

Vitamin C without SPF is a wasted ritual. Always finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Apply as the very last step, after all skincare has been absorbed.
